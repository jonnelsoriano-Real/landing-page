10 Landing Page Trends to Try

Use one clear headline and one main button. Keep sections short. Add real photos instead of stock images. Offer a dark mode. Use large, readable text. Show social proof near the top. Keep forms short. Use subtle motion only. Make every button easy to tap. Test on a real phone.

How to Choose the Right Colors

Pick one accent color and use it for buttons and links. Keep backgrounds neutral. Make sure text has strong contrast. Match the color to the mood: blue for trust, green for growth, red for urgency, purple for creativity.

Make Your Page Load Faster

Resize images before uploading them. Use JPG for photos and SVG for icons. Avoid heavy videos at the top. Skip libraries you do not need. Keep your CSS and JavaScript small.
